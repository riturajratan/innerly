Homepage discovery

Visitors can switch between three clearly labelled sample-report views, explore six feelings including relationships, and interact with two country cards inspired by the supplied reference. The country cards use World Happiness Report 2026 published life-evaluation averages: expand the top ten, choose among 147 countries/territories, and view dated three-year averages with exact values and 95% confidence intervals. Changing the dropdown updates only the country-detail chart; it never rebuilds or animates the top-ten list. Source and period remain visible. These are public life evaluations, not mental-health or personality rankings, and never affect individual scores.

Each feeling has an original code-native illustration and three clickable bars depicting a clearly labelled fictional week. These are educational examples, not the visitor’s answers or a new scoring instrument. The relationship panel links to NHS relationship and wellbeing guidance. Large start actions appear in the hero, report section and final invitation.

Save and resume now

Save & take a break stores a versioned, language-specific draft in this browser only, after a clear shared-device notice and explicit choice. It resumes at the first unanswered question and then saves further changes locally. Removing a draft leaves completed reports untouched. Finishing clears the draft; the full report still has its own save/PDF actions. Storage failures are shown and preserve answers in the open page. No account or cloud service is implied.

Question and scoring contract

The English PHQ-4 wording, two-week timeframe and 0–3 frequency scale are retained. Published total distress ranges are 0–2 normal/minimal, 3–5 mild, 6–8 moderate and 9–12 severe. A GAD-2 or PHQ-2 subscore ≥3 indicates further assessment; it is not a diagnosis. The six context items and 36 personality items never enter these totals. PHQ-4 instrument · Official scoring manual and reproduction/translation terms.

PHQ/GAD measures were developed by Robert L. Spitzer, Janet B. W. Williams, Kurt Kroenke and colleagues with an educational grant from Pfizer. PHQ-4 publication: Kroenke, Spitzer, Williams & Löwe (2009), Psychosomatics 50:613–621. The manual identifies these instruments as public domain. This does not validate Innerly's combined questionnaire or its editorial translations.

Before expanding clinical claims: arrange qualified clinical review of interpretation, escalation, accessibility and translated instruments. Keep support guidance available without payment. Do not add self-harm items until a clinically reviewed response and country-specific crisis-resource flow exists. The current four-item screen does not assess immediate safety.

API boundaries for implementation
  • POST /checkins: authenticated JWT + explicit cloud consent + instrument version + answers keyed by stable IDs. Validate against the server-side schema; return record ID and recomputed report.
  • GET /checkins/:id: enforce ownership, return the selected user's record only.
  • DELETE /checkins/:id: authenticated owner request, confirmed deletion of record and associated private PDF, followed by the documented backup-retention policy.
  • POST /orders: accept SKU and supported currency, never accept a trusted price or entitlement from the browser.
  • POST /webhooks/razorpay (and PayPal if used directly): verify raw-body signature, deduplicate provider event ID, verify captured amount/currency/order, and grant entitlement once.
  • GET /reports/:id/download: owner and entitlement checks for the future paid export; issue a short-lived private download.
